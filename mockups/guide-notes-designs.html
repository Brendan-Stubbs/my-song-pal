Guide Notes — 10 UX Redesigns

Interactive mockups for placing guide notes on the metronome. Every demo below is clickable. Sample passage: 4 bars · 4/4. The goal: a beginner guitarist can place a note on the right beat without ever opening a nested menu.

★

My pick: Design 1 — Inline Step Sequencer

It removes the current nested popover entirely: beats and subdivisions become visible columns you tap directly, exactly like a drum machine. A global resolution switch keeps it simple by default (one tap per beat) while still supporting eighths, sixteenths and triplets. It reuses the existing fretboard pitch picker, so it's the lowest-risk change with the biggest clarity win. This is the one implemented in the app.
